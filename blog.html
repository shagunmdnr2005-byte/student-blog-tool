How to Calculate CGPA

CGPA is an important part of a student's academic life. Many students find CGPA calculation confusing. Here we expalin it in a simple and easy way.

What is CGPA?

CGPA stands for Cumulative Grade Point Average It shows the overall academic performance of a student.

CGPA Formula

CGPA = Total Grade Points / Totak SUbjects

EXample of CGPA Calculation

If a student scores grade points 8, 7,9,and 8 in four subjects:
CGPA = (8 + 7 + 9 + 8) / 4 = 8.0

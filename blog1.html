HOw to calculate CGPA

Simple guide for students

What is CGPA?

CGPA ka full form hota hai Cummulative Grade Point Average Ye student ke overall academic performance ko show karta hai.

CGPA calculate karne ka Formula

CGPA = (sum of all SGPA) / Number of Semesters

Example

Agr aapke SGPA hain: 7.5,8.0,7.8,8.2
CGPA = (7.5 + 8.0 + 7.8 + 8.2) / 4 = 7.87

CGPA to Percentage

Approx Formula:
Percentage = CGPA * 9.5

Example: CGPA = 8.0 Percentage = 8.0 * 9.5 =
76%
